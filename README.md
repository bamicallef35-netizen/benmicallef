### Ben Micallef

I work on a practical, 21st-century cybernetics: small machines whose behaviour comes from a tight loop with the world, built with current, cheap hardware and tested so the claims hold. I founded [Sentinus](https://sentinus-labs.vercel.app), which builds autonomy for vehicles that cannot use GPS. Before that: a DPhil at Oxford on cybernetics and avant-garde language, an MRes in architecture and digital theory at the Bartlett, and a BA in Philosophy and Comparative Literature at Berkeley. I played for the Malta national team and competed in NCAA Division I.

**[umwelt-engine](https://github.com/benmicallef/umwelt-engine)** — a no_std Rust runtime, zero unsafe, cross-compiled to Cortex-M4F, that turns raw sensor streams into compact typed signs and gossips them over a GPS-free mesh. The representation layer under a policy, not another policy.

**[grey-walter-tortoise](https://github.com/benmicallef/grey-walter-tortoise)** — W. Grey Walter's 1949 tortoise rebuilt as heap-free C++ firmware, with a [browser simulation](https://benmicallef.github.io/grey-walter-tortoise/) proven identical to the firmware by a parity harness.

**[viab](https://github.com/benmicallef/viab)** · **[colloquy](https://github.com/benmicallef/colloquy)** · **[viable-system-model](https://github.com/benmicallef/viable-system-model)** · **[offline-edge-node](https://github.com/benmicallef/offline-edge-node)** · **[responsive-devices](https://github.com/benmicallef/responsive-devices)** — stigmergic construction after TERMES and R&Sie(n); a conversation you can bore, after Pask; Beer's VSM as a tested library; an air-gapped inference node with the air gap enforced; and six speculative devices, each tied to a parts list.

What connects them: Walter, Ashby, Pask and Beer asked what a system needs in order to keep going on its own. François Roche's architecture — R&Sie(n), later New-Territories — put the same question to buildings, as protocols run under uncertainty; I am writing a book on that work. Autonomy without GPS is the same question put to a vehicle. Each repository says what it can and cannot show.
